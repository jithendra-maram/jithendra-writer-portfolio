Article · Society · Writing sample 01

The Great Story

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

A RTI C L E - 1 The Great Story Written by the student mind TOPICS COVERED Student's Struggle Government Negligence Priority Rights STUDENT'S STRUGGLE In this present era, only one type of mindset is struggling to earn and learn. Only one mindset is struggling to grow, one mindset is struggling to think, one mindset is struggling to do, one mindset is struggling to laugh and hide their sadness. This mindset belongs to the student. The student doesn't have the proper infrastructure to do their work, and they don't have the necessary resources. "The only reason I write this is to make the students' voices reach all audiences." GOVERNMENT NEGLIGENCE The government uses public money as personal wealth and treats the public like puppets. They are deeply neglecting Indian citizens. If we showcase our talent in other countries, the government complains to the public that we are only interested in • • • serving foreign nations. Yet, if we show a strong interest in doing something for our own country, they label us as anti-nationals. Because of this, I must confidently ask: what is the government actually accomplishing by constantly telling these stories? Will they just continue to consume public money? "QUESTION THE GOVERNMENT. PUBLIC, RAISE YOUR VOICE AND DEMAND YOUR RIGHTS." PRIORITY RIGHTS In our country, rights are like website coupons—only a few use them properly. To be frank, not just a few, but very few. We have many rights to question the government, yet we act like puppets and just laugh at political leaders. It's a pity to laugh at the very politicians we elected. We are the ones who choose our leaders, yet we blindly vote based on local caste and religious affiliations. Afterwards, we troll them online, saying they aren't suited for the role. It is truly a pity. We are in a generation where technology is developing at an unimaginable rate. Think before you vote and make decisions; don't blindly follow religion and caste. The Constitution provides rules and rights for us; use them with knowledge. As the youth of this nation, we need to serve the country with great minds. CONCLUSION This is not just a story or an article; it is a life lesson that everyone in the nation should learn. "FIGHT FOR OUR COUNTRY, NOT IN THE NAME OF RELIGION." "FIGHT FOR OUR RIGHTS, NOT IN THE WRONG WAY." "THIS HAPPENS ONLY WHEN THE STUDENT RAISES THEIR VOICE." J A I H I N D

Article · Civic life · Writing sample 02

Throwing Health

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

A RTI C L E - 2 Throwing Health Written by the student mind TOPICS COVERED Public Negotiation Basic Civic Sense Wasting Money PUBLIC NEGOTIATION We live in a democratic country with a population of 1.4 billion. Yet, we still litter on the roads and spoil the streets. What do we gain from doing this—tons of litter or tons of scrap? Several countries with much smaller populations are implementing cleanliness measures beautifully. The main issue is that the public neglects their duty, acting as though it is solely the government's job while we just continue to litter the roads. As the students of this country, we need to change the nation's mindset. We must explain the problems caused by this negligence. "GREAT COUNTRIES ARE BUILT FROM CLEAN SOCIETIES, NOT FROM MERE NEGOTIATION." • • • BASIC CIVIC SENSE We constantly hear the phrase, "India is a developing country." But where is the development? This question must be answered by the public. Our country has an 80% literacy rate, and even rural areas boast a literacy rate of around 77%. Yet, we still lack basic civic sense. Even when there is a dustbin right next to us, we choose to litter around it. If someone litters around a bin, others are quick to call them a "bloody illiterate." But often, the people making these comments are doing the exact same thing, expecting someone else to clean it up. What is the use of this hypocrisy? Just question yourself. "LOOK AT YOURSELF BEFORE JUDGING OTHERS. DO YOUR OWN JOB BEFORE QUESTIONING THE REST." WASTING MONEY In our country, the government spends too much money on freebies and unwanted bills. Yet, we continue to litter everywhere on the roads and streets. Consequently, the government has to spend even more money just to clean those roads and streets. We do not even know how much they actually spend on this cleaning. We do not have the right to question the government about these bills or how much they spend, because we are the reason for this wasted expenditure. If we use bins and toilets properly and maintain basic civic sense, only then will we earn the right to question the government when they raise these types of bills. CONCLUSION Finally, this is only a minor problem if we plan properly and act using basic civic sense. "PROPER HABITS BUILD A HEALTHY SOCIETY." J A I H I N D

Money with Money — An Idea of Earning Money

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

O P I N I O N E S SAY Money with Money - An Idea of Earning Money By M. Jithendra In India, there are countless problems regarding justice, governance, and law. However, our country is currently facing some of the smoothest, most silent crimes: counterfeiting and illegal gambling. This cycle always starts from a person's anxiety and ends with their greed. There are many illegal activities in our country, but why are gambling and counterfeiting uniquely dangerous? It is because so many people have been conditioned to believe that all their problems can be easily solved by money. Yes, it is true; that is the fact in our country. Even education is sold if you have sufficient money. However, the core problem is the society, not just the person involved in the crime. We are living in a society that treats money as something greater than anything else. That societal pressure is exactly why the desperation for quick wealth increases rapidly, leading people directly into counterfeiting and gambling. "Money is a paper of addiction. Once we are addicted, it becomes a serious disease."

Opinion essay · Human stories · Writing sample 05

Loneliness — Not A Curse

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

O P I N I O N E S SAY Loneliness – Not A Curse By M. Jithendra The word "lonely" describes a solitary journey. Everyone's life usually involves a group of people to share feelings with. However, some people do not have that network to share their emotions. These individuals are what we call lonely people. DESCRIBING THE LIFE OF AN ALONE PERSON The life of an alone person is actually quite interesting. They start their day calmly. As they move through the day, they have so many things they want to share with everyone, but they simply cannot find someone to share those feelings with. For sensitive people, this kind of isolation can lead to severe health issues, while others simply continue with their daily routine. HOW TO OVERCOME LONELINESS Overcoming loneliness can be a simple process. At the very start of the day, make an effort to speak with someone near you. That small interaction makes you feel better equipped to move on with your day. Do not become an extreme introvert, as it can make you weak and unhealthy. Sitting in absolute silence and refusing to speak to anyone will only create severe problems for your future. HOW TO EMBRACE LONELINESS Continuing with loneliness is sometimes the best option for those who are naturally uninterested in constant social interaction or public speaking. Instead, they can move forward through solo traveling, exploring, and researching. Succeeding in these solitary pursuits will definitely give people something to talk about in the future. "So, do not take loneliness as a curse; one day, loneliness will make great things."

Opinion essay · Education · Writing sample 06

Rally with Papers — The Student's Life

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

O P I N I O N E S SAY Rally with Papers – The Student's Life By M. Jithendra A person's life begins with a birth certificate—just a piece of paper. As we grow up, we require an n-number of documents, which are also just papers. These are required for our daily living and existence. But what is the use of collecting unnecessary certificates in the education lifecycle? Question yourself. THE CERTIFICATION TRAP It is like a fisherman's trap. But here, the students are the fish, and the fishermen are the companies and related faculty. This trap begins with a single promise: a certification can change your life. One fine day, first-year students go to a seminar or a course-related meetup. After the seminar, the stage members discuss their certifications, exploit the students' weaknesses for cash, and leave. Once the course actually begins, no staff members deliver proper lectures. The students' money is wasted, and after completion, they receive a valueless certificate. The students believe that this certificate is an achievement. They follow this path for every course blindly, and one day, after completing their graduation, they realize that this entire certificate journey was useless. This is exactly what is happening inside colleges today. REAL CERTIFICATION What is a real certification? A certification is not about getting a piece of paper for a course. A real certification means you actually learn something. If merely "getting" a paper was the same as learning, then everyone in our country would be a doctorate in their field. So, please focus on acquiring a new skill and becoming a master in it. No certificate can match who you are when you have true expertise. If you absolutely must have a real certificate, you need to pursue real-time internships, as they are highly valuable. "Don't depend on papers. If you depend on your knowledge, then you will be independent in your life."

Agriculture — The Root of India

By M. Jithendra · Original writing

O P I N I O N E S SAY Agriculture — The Root of India By M. Jithendra At 7:00 AM, a normal person starts their routine with breakfast. They go to the office at 9:00 AM, drink coffee or tea at 10:00 AM, and head to the mess at 1:00 PM to eat their favorite food. They go home at 5:00 PM, prepare or order their favorite dish for dinner, and complete their day with satisfaction. Why mention all of this? Because a normal person working for an organization or a company is perfectly happy, enjoying their favorite meals every single day. But at the exact same time, a farmer—who is popularly known as the "backbone of India," the "hero of India," and the "food creator of India"—is suffering just to eat for the day. Why is this happening? Who is the reason behind it? Politicians? Businessmen? Tell me! We are living in a society where Artificial Intelligence is spreading like a fragrance, and everyone in the country is using advanced technology. Yet, in our villages, farmers are still using oxen to cultivate the land because they lack access to proper tools. The problems of farmers and their suicide rates are increasing, but they still receive a profound lack of support from the government. The one who cultivates food for the entire nation is financially struggling and lacks food himself. Why does this always happen to Indian farmers? "A country with the second-best production of food, but where the farmer gets the least food on his plate."
